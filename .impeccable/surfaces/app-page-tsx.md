## Direction contract

THESIS: Victor as a live control plane on phosphor glass. He is the leader node at the centre and his work runs as tracks through concentric rings. This refuses the category default of a dark portfolio with a photo hero and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground is a green-tinted near-black (#020604, panels #08110c). Text is #d8e8de, dim #6f8f7c, afterglow #1f5c3a.
- Phosphor green #4dff8f appears only on lit, live things. Amber #e8c45c means warning or degraded.
- Panels are square-cornered with hairline rules. Status is drawn by stroke: solid means live, dashed means maintained, gapped means archived.
- Mono is for machine data and a clean sans is for prose. Glow belongs to the hero and the terminal only.

STORY: The visitor sees a senior systems engineer whose output is real and countable (15 systems, 7 papers, 39 posts, 150 notes), believes the depth, and either contacts him or opens a track to read.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Left 5/12: a typed status line (leader: victor-bona · status: Ready), the one-liner at display scale, and a lit `contact` command as the primary action.
- Right 7/12: an SVG ring display with Victor at the centre and rings for Systems, Papers, Writing and Compendium with real counts. Hover or focus isolates a track; heartbeat pulses run along real links.
- Nav carries the page addresses P100–P600 and a ⌘K terminal.

FORM: Control Plane — Phosphor Edition. User steer over the bolder re-roll 2, raised by Event Display (rings), Teletext (page addresses), Coptic (only data decorates) and Variable Font Specimen (lit = heavier). Seed key c056d5c7.
